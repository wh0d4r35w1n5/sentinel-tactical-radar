01Executive summary

Sentinel Tactical Radar is a production autonomous trading system for cryptocurrency markets. It scans a live multi-venue universe, scores candidates through a statistically-fused signal stack, sizes positions on measured evidence, protects every position with a three-daemon defense mesh, and journals every decision for audit.

The honest position. The engineering is ahead of the evidence. Sentinel's machinery — evidence-gated scoring, regime-conditional strategy weighting, empirical-Kelly sizing, inventory-aware exposure control, and a self-custody on-chain execution lane — is institutional-grade in design. Live realized performance to date is net negative (−$54.80 all-time across 232 closes at the time of writing), which this plan reports plainly rather than disguising. The business case rests on a deliberate sequence: prove the edge on a small live book → scale only what survives measurement → productize the infrastructure.

Operating principle: profit is the only metric. Never win-rate alone, never gross before net. Every gate, every grade, every roadmap item is subordinate to net dollars after fees, slippage and funding.

What exists today

  • Futures lane — Bitget USDT-M perpetuals, live since Oct 2026: 20-signal scan board, 17-key statistical fusion, longs-only mandate, bounded inventory skew, Kelly-negative vetoes.
  • On-chain lane — self-custody Solana wallet trading DEX candidates via Jupiter (gasless-capable), with Rugcheck screening, purse caps, and software trails. Every fill is a solscan-verifiable signature.
  • Protection mesh — three daemons: execution supervisor, 10s protection watcher (band-clamped stop repair, staggered TP ladders), and a ~5s liquidation guard (crash brake, liq-zone trims, naked-stop synthesis).
  • Wealth vault — 25% carry on net-new equity highs, high-water-mark gated, physically segregated from the tradable book.
  • Attribution layer — "Pimp City": every fill assigned to a persona, per-pimp and per-girl P&L, vault-carry KPIs, form/mood evolution — the ledger that answers "who made the money."
  • Ops surface — live dashboard, Telegram command-and-control, VIP signal ingestion as confluence, TradingView webhooks, integrity checksums on all state.

03Edge machinery

Sentinel's stack is built around one desk rule: never widen the proof bar to widen the funnel. Components compete on measured marginal contribution, and measured-negative cells lose their bullets.

The 17-key fusion

  • Serial demeaning — each component minus its ~30-cycle EMA; only the surprise carries signal.
  • Cross-sectional z — every key z-scored across the board each cycle (±4 clamp); ranks over raw units.
  • Residual weighting — Bayesian ridge of the z-map onto realized 24h alpha, λ=30 shrunk to hand priors. The momentum/volume/surge triple-count dies here — each key earns only its marginal lift.
  • Evidence ramp — blend = n/(n+300), capped 0.7. Hand-tuned prior is the floor until sealed evals accumulate.

05Market & business model

The opportunity

Retail crypto trading remains dominated by discretionary losses and unsystematic bots. The durable gap is not signal generation — it is audited, cost-aware, self-evaluating execution. Sentinel's moat, if it earns one, is the evidence pipeline: every signal sealed and labeled, every strategy scored on its own realized record, every fee accounted.
