- **Nobody freelances**: no agent acts outside the plan. Longs-only, convicted cells dead, breakers bind. La famiglia takes down scores together or not at all.
- **Adapt within the plan**: when the vault layout changed, the crew changed the plan — never the discipline. Epoch resets are our sanctioned pivots: documented, attributed, honest.
